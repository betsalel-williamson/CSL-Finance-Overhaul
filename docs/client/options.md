# Options

Open **Options → Finance Overhaul**.

| Option                                          | Default                     | What it changes                                                                  |
| ----------------------------------------------- | --------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Live Stock Exchange ticker                      | On                          | Off brings back the game's original boards                                       |
| Stock Exchange ticker shows                     | As the change pills show it | As the pills (% or ₡), change %, or price and ₡ change                           |
| Show the LED ticker in info views               | On                          | Keep the ticker in info views or hide it there                                   |
| LED ticker brightness                           | Bright                      | Classic, Bright or Brightest (daylight)                                          |
| Orders stay open for (new orders)               | 90 days                     | 30, 60, 90, 120, 180 or 365 days                                                 |
| Link the Investments list and the Stock Monitor | On                          | One sort and one % / ₡ for both (and the LED boards); off gives the list its own |
| Order and bond notices in Chirper               | On                          | All mod chirps on or off                                                         |
| Also chirp every bond coupon                    | Off                         | A chirp for every coupon, not just repayments                                    |
| Show a dialog when a local company goes public  | On                          | The IPO dialog; the chirp comes either way                                       |

## Local IPOs

Every value [Local IPOs](./local-ipos.md) uses is an option, in four groups: **when companies list**
(Stock Exchange level, squares of a kind, listings per map tile, the most at once and in a year),
**which businesses can list** (healthy days, size, land value, industry area level, factory
production), **prices** (offering window, price from earnings, first-day jump) and **buy-outs and
closing**. Each row has a slider and a box to type the value.

**Presets** at the top: **Less likely**, **Default** and **More likely**, or **Save as…** to keep
your own values under a name (Delete removes one). Each group also has its own Less likely, Defaults
and More likely buttons. A change applies from the next game day.

**Copy build info** puts the version on your clipboard; paste it into bug reports. Stock Monitor
settings are under its own gear button (see [Stock Monitor](./stock-monitor.md)).
