# Typed share amount

In **Economy → Investments**, click **Buy** or **Sell** on any stock. In the **Invest in** dialog,
click the share count next to the slider and type the number you want.

1. The slider and **Price** follow as you type.
2. Press **Enter**, **Tab**, or click anywhere else to apply the number.
3. Press **Escape** to put back the number you started with.
4. Numbers above what you can afford (or own, when selling) snap to the maximum.
5. Enter and Escape only affect the number while you are typing. Click **Buy** / **Sell** to trade.

The slider and **Price** update. 2. Numbers above what you can afford (or own, when selling) snap to
the maximum. 3. Enter only applies the number while you are typing. Click **Buy** / **Sell** to
trade.

The slider and **−** / **+** still work, and the field follows them. This is a UI-only change; your
save is not affected.
