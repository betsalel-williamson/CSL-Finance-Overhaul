# About this guide

**Prepared for:** Cities: Skylines I mayors who own Financial Districts and want the Stock Exchange
to behave more like city treasury management than a day-trading minigame.

**Product:** Finance Overhaul

This client guide covers install, the MVP probe surface, and the **Index** stock (equal-weight city
index fund). Bonds, commodities, sector ETFs, automation, and IPOs land in later releases — see
[Market analogs](../features/market-analogs.md).
