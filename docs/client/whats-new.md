# What's new in 0.2.0

The second release adds two big features and polishes almost everything from 0.1.0. Your saves carry
over: nothing to do but update.

## New

- **[One Investments tab](./investments-tab.md):** Economy → Investments is now one list of every
  stock, with your holdings, average cost, gain and open orders on each line, and an overview beside
  it with five views: Market, Portfolio, Heatmap, City and Feed.
- **[Local IPOs](./local-ipos.md):** a business that has done well in your city for a long time can
  go public. Buy in at the offering price, follow it from its building, and watch it trade.
- **Chirps tab** in the Invest dialog: what Chirper said about that stock lately (see
  [Buying, selling and orders](./trading-and-orders.md#chirps)).
- **Buy | Sell tabs** in the Invest dialog: turn an open dialog from buying to selling without
  closing it.
- **Stock Monitor Orders tab:** every open order, how far its stock is from the trigger, and when it
  ends.

## Changed

- **% or points, everywhere at once:** a click on any change pill switches every pill between % and
  ₡ (points), and the Stock Exchange's LED boards follow. The Investments tab and the Stock Monitor
  share one sort and one % / ₡ by default; Options can unlink them.
- **Stock Monitor:** a click on a stock opens the game's Invest dialog with its chart and stats; Esc
  closes the window; it is solid under the mouse; its list fades at the edges when there is more to
  scroll.
- **City bonds** look like the game's loan cards, pay their coupon every week like loans do, have
  terms in weeks (52, 104 or 260) and a name you can type.
- **The game's look:** the mod's windows, tabs, pills, charts and numbers use the game's own
  colours, fonts and controls.

## Fixed

- The Buy dialog no longer ignores clicks when there is nothing left to buy.
- "1 share", not "1 shares".
- The LED ticker follows a Stock Exchange that was turned or moved.
- Two copies of the mod switched on: the mod asks which to keep.
