The Stock Exchange LED boards now follow the change pills: click any % / ₡ pill (in the Investments
list, the overview or the Stock Monitor) and the boards switch between "+3.22%" and price with the ₡
change too. This is the new default in Options → "Stock Exchange ticker shows"; a fixed change % or
price and ₡ change can still be picked there.
