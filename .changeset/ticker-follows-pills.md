The Stock Exchange LED boards now follow the change pills: click any % / ₡ pill (in the Stock
Monitor, or the Investments list, which is linked to it by default) and the boards switch between
"+3.22%" and price with the ₡ change too. This is the new default in Options → "Stock Exchange
ticker shows"; a fixed change % or price and ₡ change can still be picked there.
