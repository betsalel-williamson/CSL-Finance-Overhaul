Bonds you owe now look like the game's loan cards: each bond is a card with its amount, term,
interest, next coupon and its date, the day it is repaid, the coupons left and the Total that paying
now costs, with a Pay Now button. This also fixes the repayment date running under the Repay button
on large bonds. Each bond has a name box: it starts as "Bond #63" and you can type what the money is
for, such as a new hospital. The name is saved with the city.
