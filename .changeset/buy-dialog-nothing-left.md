"csl-finance-overhaul": patch

Opening Buy for a stock the city already held all it may of (from the Stock Monitor) gave a dialog
that ignored every mouse click; only Escape closed it. Buy is now greyed out with a tooltip when
there is nothing left to buy, and a Buy dialog opened anyway turns to Sell (or closes if the city
holds none). Tab groups also no longer miss a quick second click.
