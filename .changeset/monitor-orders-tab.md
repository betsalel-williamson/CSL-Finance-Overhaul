Stock Monitor, Orders tab: every open order is listed under its stock, with its side and type, the
shares and trigger price, the days left, and how far today's price is from the trigger. Orders whose
type is switched off in Options are shown muted. The tab shows how many orders are open, and has its
own sort: nearest to its trigger, soonest to expire, or by symbol.
