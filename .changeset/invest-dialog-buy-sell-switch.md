The Buy and Sell dialog now has Buy | Sell tabs above the order types: you can turn an open dialog
from buying to selling (and back) without closing it and opening another. Sell is greyed out, with a
tooltip saying why, when the city holds no shares of that stock. Every one-of-several choice in the
mod (the dialog's order types, the Stock Monitor's All / Portfolio / Orders and 7D / 30D, the bond
term) is now drawn the same way: drawn as the game's tab strip, the chosen one pressed.
