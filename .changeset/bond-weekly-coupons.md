"csl-finance-overhaul": minor
---

City bonds now pay their coupon every week, as the game's loans do, instead of every 30 days. The
Loans tab shows a bond's Weekly Cost and Time Left in weeks, and the coupons now appear week by week
in Economy under loan expenses (before, that line usually read ₡0 between monthly coupons). Bonds
you already owe are converted when the city loads: same amount, rate and name, the rest of the term
in weekly coupons counted from the last coupon paid. The repayment day moves by at most three days,
and the interest left to pay is never more than before.
