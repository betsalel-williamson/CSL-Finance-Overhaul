When two copies of Finance Overhaul are switched on (the Workshop copy and a local one), the mod
asks which to keep and switches the other off, so orders, bond payments and the Index are never
handled twice. The question waits until any other open dialog is closed, so it is never lost behind
one; closing it with Escape keeps both and asks again when a city next loads. The copy switched off
shows Off in Content Manager straight away.
