Clicking a stock in the Stock Monitor now opens the game's Buy / Sell dialog for it, instead of a
separate detail page in the Monitor. The dialog shows what that page did: 7D / 30D tabs on its
chart, the change since yesterday next to the current value, and the stock's stats (yesterday, the
high and low, shares owned, average cost, value held, gain and open orders). The dialog stays
centred on screen as it grows.
