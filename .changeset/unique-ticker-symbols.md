"csl-finance-overhaul": patch

Ticker symbols are now unique and the same in every language: ChirperCrypto shows as CHC (it no
longer shares CCr with Chirpy Cruises) on the LED boards, in the Stock Monitor and in Your
Portfolio. Symbols are keyed by the game's internal stock names, and stocks added by future updates
or other mods get their own symbol automatically.
