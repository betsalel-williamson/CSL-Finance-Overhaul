Local IPOs (experimental): a listed company whose headquarters is demolished, abandoned or burns
down now moves to another business of its kind and keeps trading. If none can take it in, its price
falls for 90 days while it keeps looking, then it closes and the city is paid the last price for its
shares. Chirper tells you at each step. Before, nothing happened and the company traded on with no
building behind it.
